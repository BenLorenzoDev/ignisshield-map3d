How to set concrete, mixed or plywood

Choose Custom to use each building’s inputs, then edit Fuel load density (Mb) below. Editing shared building values requires an editor account. Trial presets apply their own values to every building.

Model classFuel load (MJ/m²)Factor
1 · Concrete / lower fuelBelow 7000.35
2 · Mixed / medium fuel700 to below 1,1000.85
3 · Wood, nipa, plywood / higher fuel1,100 or more1.40

These are the model’s assumed classes. Fuel load includes burnable contents, walls and roof. A traced outline cannot identify construction material, and an entered value is not automatically field-verified. Do not infer fuel load from appearance alone.

    Legend
    People (during a fire)
    • Walking: one representative per departing building; nearby figures are thinned to keep the map readable. The live counts include everyone.
    • ✓Reached safety: people who arrived at the main road or a safe area.
    • !No safe route: people whose way out is blocked in the simulation. This does not mean they died.
    • ?Path unknown: the map has no usable connection from their home to a road or alley. The map may be incomplete.

    How to run a fire
    1. Click any building on the map.
    2. Check its nine inputs, or pick a Trial preset.
    3. Press Start fire here. Watch it spread; the outputs (Y1–Y8) appear at the bottom.
    Missing houses: Satellite → Trace building. Alleys: Draw path. Escape routes: Routes. Past results: Run log.
    Path (field survey)
    Used by Routes: walking routes can use every path; fire-truck routes only paths marked for cars and at least as wide as the truck needs.
